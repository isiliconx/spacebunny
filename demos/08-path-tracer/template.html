What you're looking at

Unidirectional path tracing, one sample per pixel per frame, accumulated into a float buffer. Direct lighting is next-event estimated against the HDR environment with a luminance-CDF importance sampler, combined with BSDF sampling via MIS, so the image converges in tens of samples rather than thousands.
