u″ = −2g·u′ − (ω² + g²)u   ⇒   u = A·e−gt·sin(ωt + φ)
x = Σk Ak·sin(ω0rkt + φk)    y = x delayed by δk    ω = ω0·rk

Eight damped oscillators — four harmonics on each of two orthogonal axes, sharing a frequency ladder ω0·rk — are summed into a pen that never quite repeats. Each mode is A·e−γt·sin(ωt + φ), so the exponential decay is the dynamics rather than a fade applied afterwards. Integrated with RK4 at a fixed Δ = 1/1200 s; every sample is additively splatted into an RGBA32F buffer multiplied by a decay factor every frame — the figure is literally its own decaying memory.
